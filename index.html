The work schools already do, with less paper.

Fedena’s modules follow the school year, from the first enquiry to the report card.

Ad

Student admission

Track enquiries, keep admission details digital, and shape forms around how your school enrolls students.

At

Timetable and attendance

Assign subjects, rooms, and teachers, mark attendance, and give families a clear view of the day.

Ex

Exams and gradebook

Run examinations, calculate grades, and publish digital report cards students and parents can open.

Fe

Fees

Set fee structures, apply discounts or late fines, and see who still has a balance outstanding.

On

Online classes

Connect teaching to video tools such as Google Meet, Zoom, and BigBlueButton when class moves online.

Hr

People and campus

Cover staff and payroll alongside hostel, library, and transport when the school needs those modules.

Who it’s for

One school. A view for every role.

Each person signs in to the work that belongs to them — academics, fees, announcements, or a child’s progress.

  • AdministratorsAdmissions, fees, reports, approvals, and the school calendar.
  • TeachersAttendance, assignments, timetables, and the gradebook.
  • StudentsClasses, homework, exam schedules, and results.
  • ParentsAttendance, fees, announcements, and more than one child on one account.

A quieter school day.

The same information moves from the office to the classroom to a parent’s phone.

Open the day

Teachers see the timetable, mark attendance, and families get the update without a separate notice.

Teach and assess

Close the loop
